Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Overview

CarromGo ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile Carrom game. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Information We Collect

  • Anonymous usage analytics via Google Firebase Analytics (game modes played, session length, settings used). This data is not linked to your personal identity.
  • Crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics (device model, OS version, stack traces). This helps us fix bugs and is not linked to your personal identity.
  • Game settings stored locally on your device (difficulty, audio, timer preferences).

What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or phone number.
  • We do not require login or account creation.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or location.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Your Control

You can disable anonymous analytics at any time from Settings → About → Share Anonymous Analytics.

If you are in the EU, UK, or EEA, analytics collection is disabled by default until you opt in.

Children's Privacy

CarromGo is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data Retention and Deletion

Analytics data is retained in aggregated form by Firebase Analytics for 14 months, then automatically deleted. Crash reports are retained for 90 days. You can request deletion of any data associated with your device by contacting us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
